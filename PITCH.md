## We Also Caught a Real Problem on the Old Site

While building, we found that two of your photos (the About page and Services page hero images) have old marketing text baked directly into the image file from a previous design — it's not something you'd notice unless you looked closely, but it made those sections look cluttered and hard to read once we tried using them. We swapped in two of your other clean photos instead (a technician treating a yard, and an inspection photo) so nothing was lost — just worth knowing for your own future use of those two files.
