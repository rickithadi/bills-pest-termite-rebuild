## Trust Convention
Primary customer: Phoenix-area homeowner or property manager who has spotted pest/termite evidence (mud tubes, droppings, scorpions, pigeons) and is anxious about property damage or safety, comparing 2-3 local providers quickly, often on a phone.
Buying context: Moderately urgent, risk-averse, wants to feel the company is licensed, established, and will not pressure them into unnecessary work ("no unnecessary pressure" is literally the source site's own tagline).
Trust is: the primary conversion driver.
Category trust conventions: Navy/blue + white with red/warm accent CTAs, real team and technician photography, visible review counts and star ratings, license numbers, "family-owned since [year]" framing. All three competitors researched (Truly Nolen, Blue Sky, Burns) use variants of this same blue-trust palette and badge-forward layout.
Aesthetic risk: None planned. The rebuild stays within the conventional trust-blue direction the category (and this client's own current site) already uses.
Verdict: Safe. Differentiate through sharper typography (Rubik/Montserrat pairing vs. generic Divi defaults), better editorial use of the client's own team photography, a cleaner information architecture, clearer exposed pricing, and stronger storytelling — not through a different industry-coded aesthetic.
